Six Players Secure 2027 PGA Tour Cards Ahead of Korn Ferry Tour Finale

Michael Johnson earns his first card a decade after turning professional, while Tommy Morrison advances in the same season he joined through PGA Tour University.

Simon Bale|October 8, 2026
Categories: Tours
Keywords: Korn Ferry Tour, PGA Tour
Reading time: 3 minutes
  • Six more players have secured PGA Tour membership for 2027 through the Korn Ferry Tour.
  • Thirteen players are assured of cards, leaving seven available at the season finale.
  • The Korn Ferry Tour Championship runs from 8-11 October at The Federal Club in Virginia.

Tommy Morrison, Hunter Eichhorn, Dylan Menante, Cooper Dossey, Michael Johnson and Jeremy Gandon are mathematically assured of finishing in the Korn Ferry Tour’s top 20 and earning 2027 PGA Tour membership, the PGA Tour announced on Wednesday.

The Korn Ferry Tour Championship begins today at The Federal Club in Glen Allen, Virginia, concluding on Sunday. The 25th and final event of the season will settle the remaining seven cards through the final season-long points standings. Twelve of the 13 players already assured of promotion will receive their first PGA Tour card.

Johnson’s long wait ends

Michael Johnson’s breakthrough comes a decade after he turned professional out of Auburn University in 2016. He has played eight of the past nine Korn Ferry Tour seasons, compiling 14 top-25 finishes in 24 starts this year, including a share of second at the Astara Chile Classic.

Jeremy Gandon has secured his first card after finishing 22nd last season, two places outside the promotion positions. The Frenchman won April’s LECOM Suncoast Classic, giving him a victory in successive seasons.

Cooper Dossey advances in his third season, having finished 76th in 2024 and 44th in 2025. A tied-second finish in Chile helped his cause, followed by tied-sixth and tied-fourth finishes in the opening two Korn Ferry Tour Finals events.

Morrison’s rapid rise

Tommy Morrison earned Korn Ferry Tour membership by finishing sixth in the 2026 PGA Tour University Ranking after three and a half seasons at Texas. He then recorded top-10 finishes in each of his first five starts, including victory at the Evans Scholars Invitational and two runner-up finishes.

Morrison becomes the third player, following Adrien Dumont de Chassart and Karl Vilips, to earn a PGA Tour card through the Korn Ferry Tour in the same season he received PGA Tour University membership benefits.

Hunter Eichhorn also secured promotion in his first Korn Ferry Tour season. His nine top-10 finishes include a playoff defeat to Doc Redman at the Visit Knoxville Open.

Dylan Menante advances in his second season after finishing 53rd as a rookie. His first title came at the Tulum Championship, where a closing nine-under-par 63 delivered a six-shot victory over Blades Brown.

Golf Channel will televise all four rounds from 3-6pm ET on Thursday and Friday and 1-4pm ET at the weekend. The card ceremony will stream on the Golf Channel app.

GolfToday Verdict: Morrison’s rapid promotion and Johnson’s persistence give the PGA Tour’s 2027 intake an appealing blend of fresh promise and hard-earned experience.

Alcanada Grand Final to Decide 15 DP World Tour Cards

The HotelPlanner Tour’s top players will head to Mallorca from October 29 to November 1.

Simon Bale|October 1, 2026
Categories: The Tours, Tours
Keywords: Club de Golf Alcanada, DP World Tour, HotelPlanner Tour, Road to Mallorca, Robert Trent Jones
  • The 2026 Grand Final supported by the R&A takes place at Alcanada from October 29 to November 1.
  • The 45-player field will compete for 15 DP World Tour cards.
  • Alcanada will host the Grand Final for a fifth consecutive year and sixth time overall.

Club de Golf Alcanada will host the HotelPlanner Tour’s Grand Final supported by the R&A from October 29 to November 1, with 15 DP World Tour cards at stake.

Two counting events remain before the leading 45 players in the Road to Mallorca Rankings qualify for the season finale. The 2026 schedule comprises 28 tournaments across 19 countries on three continents, culminating in the promotion contest at the Mallorca venue.

Formerly known as the Challenge Tour, the HotelPlanner Tour is Europe’s principal development circuit and a pathway to the DP World Tour. All four days of the final will receive live global television coverage through partners including Sky Sports and Golf Channel.

Blomstrand leads the Road to Mallorca

Sweden’s Christofer Blomstrand currently tops the rankings. MJ Daffue is second, with Julien Sale and Pablo Ereño completing the provisional top four.

David Horsey, Matthew Southgate, Chris Wood, Marc Warren and Barclay Brown also occupy qualifying positions as players seek to confirm their places in the final field.

Southgate’s season includes an eight-shot Swiss Challenge victory in June. He closed with a 9-under 62 at Golf Sempach to finish at 23-under and claim his maiden HotelPlanner Tour title. Warren was among the seven players tied for second place after a final-round 63.

Morrison and Penge’s success

James Morrison won last year’s Grand Final to complete his return to the DP World Tour, while JC Ritchie finished top of the Road to Mallorca Rankings.

Marco Penge, the 2023 champion at Alcanada, went on to win three DP World Tour titles in 2025. His third victory of that season, at the Spanish Open, also secured places in the 2026 Masters and Open Championship.

Penge finished second to Rory McIlroy in the Race to Dubai Rankings and earned the Seve Ballesteros Award as the 2025 Player of the Year.

The Grand Final’s roll of honour dates back to 1995. Henrik Stenson won in 2000 before going on to become an Open champion and reach world number two.

Alcanada’s coastal tournament test

Alcanada first hosted the Grand Final in 2019 and has welcomed it every year since 2022. Its Robert Trent Jones Jnr-designed par-72 layout looks towards the Mediterranean Sea and the Alcanada lighthouse, with exposed coastal winds adding to the demands of its fast greens and strategically positioned bunkers.

Ahead of its first Grand Final, the club began a €600,000 renovation programme in December 2018, with work to relay all 18 greens using bentgrass T1.

“The tournament has become an important part of the club’s identity, and we are proud to once again provide the stage for players competing to progress to the DP World Tour,” said Kristoff Both, director of golf at Club de Golf Alcanada.
