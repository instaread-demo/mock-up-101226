Witness recounts Applecrest parking lot crash that injured two: 'He was barreling'

Witness recounts Applecrest parking lot crash that injured two: 'He was barreling'

Max Sullivan   Portsmouth Herald

HAMPTON FALLS — The driver whose car struck two women at Applecrest Farm Orchards on Oct. 7 was “barreling” through the parking lot in reverse, one witness said as police continue to investigate.

Nathaniel Pepe, a photographer from Manchester, said he was in the parking lot at noon with his co-workers for a company outing when the driver of a nearby white Honda Civic reversed up over the sidewalk.

“It was only a couple inches to feet away,” Pepe said. “He hit everything on the sidewalk, smashed into a car.”

Pepe said the driver veered off the sidewalk, causing additional damage. He said the car then sped in reverse through the parking area toward Exeter Road, striking two women before hitting two more parked vehicles.

"He was barreling," Pepe said.

Pepe said the women struck were not together, each with separate parties. Pepe said he and others went to their side to help while waiting for first responders to arrive.

The first woman struck, Pepe said, was leaning against a car when the white Honda Civic scraped the side of her leg. He said her leg had become "degloved" and had some bone showing. He said a bystander immediately provided a belt to be used as a tourniquet to “stop the profuse bleeding.”

“She was still talking, she was still moving,” Pepe said. “It looked like she was in shock.”

The other woman, he said, was “laid out, face to the pavement” by the impact of the car. The women were taken to area hospitals, according to Hampton Falls police.

The incident was reported around noon Oct. 7 in the parking lot at 133 Exeter Road, according to Hampton Falls police Lt. Justin Ranauro. He said the following day he could not release details of the crash, including the condition of the two women or how the crash occurred.

The day of the crash, he said it was too early to say if charges will be filed. At this point, though, he said “there’s nothing suspicious” that police have learned about the crash. Police also said the driver was cooperative with police.

Hampton Falls police and fire departments responded to the call. Additional first responders arrived and provided medical treatment to the injured pedestrians. Hampton Falls Police and Fire Department were assisted by Seabrook Fire Department and Plaistow ALS.

Pepe said he remained at Applecrest for about an hour. He said it was difficult for some people to leave the parking lot until the ambulance had left.
