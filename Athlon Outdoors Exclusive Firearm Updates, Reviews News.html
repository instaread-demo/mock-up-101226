5.11 Marksman Shirt — Coverage Without Compromise

By Dave Bahde

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 4:03
Instaread

Working on my home range or teaching at Gunsite Academy spending eight hours or more on the range it becomes critical to protect yourself from sun exposure. Early on sunscreen seemed to do the trick, but the more time spent in the sun the more I cover up. Fabrics these days offer UPF 50+ protection with no need to “reapply” throughout the day. Mostly geared towards fisherman or boaters the colors tend to be on the bright side. That is acceptable attending a class at Gunsite, it is not as an instructor. Dress code requires a collared shirt, creases, and neat and clean functioning pockets. 5.11 Tactical’s Marksman Long Sleeve shirt fits that bill about perfectly.  

Marksman Long Sleeve Shirt

Designed to be worn tucked or covering the belt the Marksman is constructed of a light weight, breathable fabric that wicks away moisture. Vent-Tac regulates airflow keeping you cooler and far more comfortable. Mesh gussets allow for greater movement as well as ventilation keeping you cool. Dual chest pockets look sharp while remaining functional.  They can hold note pads or other items with a flap that closes maintaining the professional look.  For those long days in the sun it has a UPF 50+ protection rating.  Buttons provide a more professional look and clean lines whether tucked in or over the belt for concealment.

Testing

Over the course of a few months the Marksman was worn while on my home range during various tests. Tucked in using a duty style rig it had a very clean look, and the extra-long tail kept it in place. Movement was largely unrestricted, the gussets and Vent-Tac really made a difference. Temperatures at home were in the mid to upper 90’s and this shirt kept me dry and  cool. During concealed carry practice the shirt was wide enough to conceal my Mitch Rosen OWB holster for the Sig Sauer P226 X Carry with no printing. Buttons mean a bit more authoritative garment clearing is necessary, but with practice it was plenty fast.  This shirt was also used during a Media Direct event held at the “site” facility in upper Illinois. Testing pistols, subguns and suppressed rifles there was no restricted movement.  

Temperatures were in the. Upper 90’s with humidity at roughly the same number. This was a fantastic chance to see how well it worked in the heat, and the Marksman did a fantastic job. Never felt like I was baking in the sun, it did not stick to me, and it kept what breeze there was moving through. Moving south to Arizona in the 95-100 degree heat the Marksman was equally as effective. Less humidity for sure, but direct sunlight with little or no breeze was another serious test. Even in direct sunlight for a couple hours it remained comfortable, kept me dry, and when graced with a breeze even a little cooler. This shirt may just see some actual use teaching on the ranch this summer.

Bottom Line 

This is a very professional looking shirt with nice lines, clean fabric and pockets that remain functional. It kept me cool and protected over long hours on the range in three separate conditions. Compared to my much older 5.11 shirts these seem to fit tighter. All my older stuff is XL and still fits fine, needed to move up to 2X to accommodate my arms and chest with both the short sleeve Gunsite staff shirts and this Marksman. Of course this will vary per user but be prepared to go up a size if needed. Looking for a nice looking professional grade shirt that blocks the sun then take a close look at this Marksman Long Sleeve, you will not be disappointed.
