The Church of The Assumption, Tullaroan celebrated its 200th anniversary on Saturday. Mass was concelebrated by Fr Mark Con- don, PP Tullaroan, along with Fr Dan Bollard, retired parish priest of Thomastown and former parish priest of Tullaroan.

The Mass was attended by a full congregation of parishioners, friends and well-wishers.
