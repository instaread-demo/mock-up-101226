Putting People and Process Before Technology with Todd Dailey

  • Home
  • October 7, 2026
Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:27
Instaread

On this episode of Agency Nation Radio, we meet Todd Dailey, president and COO of Insurance Management Group in Indianapolis, Indiana. 

Dailey takes a people-first approach to technology, prioritizing people, then process, and finally technology. He believes that employees who have the right support and resources are better positioned to deliver a positive client experience. That’s why he places greater emphasis on building effective processes than on the technology itself, ensuring employees have the tools and support they need to succeed. 

“Technology should come last because it only magnifies the quality of the process underneath it,” Dailey says. “If the process is poor, the technology can make the problem worse, but if the process is strong, technology can make it more effective.”
