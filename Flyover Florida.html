Isaias Puts Panhandle on Hurricane Watch

Tropical Storm Isaias is forecast to become a hurricane and strike the northern Gulf Coast late this week, with the forecast cone stretching from eastern Louisiana to the western Panhandle. A hurricane watch runs from Bay St. Louis, Mississippi, to Indian Pass.

Forecasters expect rapid strengthening, with Isaias becoming a hurricane today and possibly reaching 110 mph by Friday, though it could weaken some before landfall. A storm surge watch stretches to Yankeetown, with 5 to 7 feet of surge possible along parts of the Mississippi, Alabama, and Panhandle coasts.

Gov. Ron DeSantis declared a state of emergency in 25 counties, including the Panhandle and Big Bend, and state emergency managers moved to Level 1, a full-scale activation.

Florida Wants Kids Under 14 Off Meta

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier asked a Pasco County judge on Wednesday to order Meta to remove users under 14 from Facebook and Instagram.

The injunction would also cap teens at two hours a day across Meta’s apps, turn off autoplay and infinite scroll, cut off messaging once limits hit, and ban ads aimed at teens.

Florida declined to join a multibillion-dollar settlement Meta reached with most states in August. The state’s filing calls that deal a “slap on the wrist” and argues it shows Meta can make the changes.

Florida wants the changes in place while its lawsuit plays out. Meta didn’t immediately comment, but has said it works hard to keep teens safe and denied wrongdoing when it settled.

Feds Seize Cuba-Bound Fuel in Florida

Federal agents seized about 500,000 gallons of biodiesel at Port Everglades that officials say was headed illegally to a sanctioned Cuban state fuel company. Another 19 shipments were stopped at the Port of Houston, bringing the total to 90 shipments worth nearly $3 million.

Officials announced the seizures on Wednesday in Sweetwater, saying the fuel was bound for Enetec S.A., a Havana-based wholesaler the Treasury Department sanctioned in July.

 Customs officials flagged the cargo in early September after combing trade and intelligence data. No charges have been announced.
